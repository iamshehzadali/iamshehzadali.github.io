Privacy Policy — LabPy IDE

Developer: Shehzad Ali
Contact: starlancer789@gmail.com

Data collection

LabPy IDE does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. The app works fully offline: all code you write, run, and save stays on your device in the app's private storage.

Advertising

LabPy IDE shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device information and your advertising ID to show relevant ads and to measure ad performance. This data is collected and shared with Google under Google's privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device's Settings (Privacy → Ads). No analytics are used, and the app has no accounts.

Local storage

Scripts you save ("My Scripts") and your settings (theme, font size, language) are stored only on your device. You can delete saved scripts at any time from within the app, and clearing the app's data removes everything.

Permissions

LabPy IDE requests the Internet permission, which is used only to load ads from Google AdMob. It does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or files outside its own storage (except when you explicitly choose to open or save a .py file).

Children

The app is an educational tool aimed at students aged 13 and older. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children; advertising data is handled by Google AdMob as described above.

Contact

If you email the developer, your email address is used only to reply to you.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this same address and inside the app (About & Privacy).
