Privacy Policy — Matric Grammar

Last updated: 5 December 2026

Matric Grammar is an English grammar learning app for Class 9 and 10 students by Shehzad Ali. The app contains the full Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board book "English Grammar and Composition 9–10" and works on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information.

Information the app collects

The app itself collects no personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics. The only data collected is your Google Advertising ID, which is collected by Google AdMob (see "Advertising" below) to show ads. Matric Grammar does not collect your name, email, location, or anything you type, read, or search in the app.

Your progress

Bookmarks and your last-opened lesson are saved only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere, and they are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app uses the internet only to load ads from Google AdMob. It requests no other permissions. All lessons, vocabulary, quizzes, and Urdu meanings are stored inside the app.

Advertising

Matric Grammar shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob collects your Google Advertising ID — a device identifier that you can reset or turn off in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads) — and shares it with Google to show you ads. No other information is collected for advertising, and no information is shared with anyone else.

Children

Matric Grammar is made for Class 9 and 10 students (ages 14–16). The app itself collects no information from anyone of any age. The only data collected is the Advertising ID, collected by Google AdMob for advertising as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
