Privacy Policy — Matric Grammar

Last updated: 21 November 2026

Matric Grammar is an English grammar learning app for Class 9 and 10 students by Shehzad Ali. The app contains the full Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board book "English Grammar and Composition 9–10" and works completely offline on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information: nothing.

Information the app collects

None. Matric Grammar does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising in the app. Matric Grammar does not sell any data — it collects none to sell.

Your progress

Bookmarks and your last-opened lesson are saved only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere, and they are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at all. It has no internet permission: all lessons, vocabulary, quizzes, and Urdu meanings are stored inside the app and work without any connection.

Advertising

Matric Grammar shows no ads and uses no advertising service. It does not use an advertising ID.

Children

Matric Grammar is made for Class 9 and 10 students (ages 14–16). It collects no information from anyone of any age — children or adults — because it collects nothing at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
