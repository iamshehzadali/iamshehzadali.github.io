Privacy Policy — Reeling Loop

Last updated: 24 November 2026

Reeling Loop is a video saver app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. In short: the app itself collects nothing about you — it has no accounts, no sign-in and no analytics. The advertising shown inside the app is served by Google AdMob, which may collect your device's advertising ID, as described below.

Information we collect

None. Reeling Loop does not collect, store or share your name, email address, location, contacts or any other personal information.

Advertising

Reeling Loop shows ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and share your device's advertising ID and other device information to show ads, measure how ads perform, and (where you have not opted out) personalise ads. This data is collected and shared with Google for advertising purposes. You can opt out of personalised ads or reset your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads). Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

How the app works

When you paste a video link and tap to save, the app fetches that video directly from the public page you linked and stores the file in your device's Download folder. The video links you paste and the videos you save never leave your device. The app does not upload anything to any server.

Permissions

The app uses internet access only to fetch the video you asked to save, and storage access (on older Android versions) only to write the saved file to your Download folder. Both are used solely for the feature you requested.

Children

Reeling Loop is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone of any age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page.
