Privacy Policy — Riazi Keybook

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Riazi Keybook is a mathematics key book app for Class 9 and 10 students by Shehzad Ali (Xzad Apps). It contains step-by-step solutions for the Punjab Curriculum and Textbook Board mathematics books — 408 questions for Class 9 and 330 questions for Class 10 — and works completely offline on your phone. This policy explains what the app does with your information: nothing.

Information the app collects

None. Riazi Keybook does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, no analytics, and no advertising in the app. Riazi Keybook does not sell any data — it collects none to sell.

Bookmarks

Bookmarks are saved only on your own phone, in the app's private storage. They are never sent anywhere, and they are deleted if you uninstall the app.

Permissions

The app requests no permissions at all. It has no internet permission: all questions, solutions, and diagrams are stored inside the app and work without any connection.

Advertising

Riazi Keybook shows no ads and uses no advertising service. It does not use an advertising ID.

Children

Riazi Keybook is made for Class 9 and 10 students (ages 14–16). It collects no information from anyone of any age — children or adults — because it collects nothing at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
