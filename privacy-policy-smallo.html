Privacy Policy — Smallo

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Smallo is an image resizer by Shehzad Ali (Xzad Apps). Your photos are resized on your phone and are never uploaded to any server. This policy explains what the app — and the advertising service it uses — does with your information.

Information the app collects

None. Smallo does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information itself. There is no account, no sign-in, and no analytics collected by the app. Smallo does not sell any data — it collects none to sell.

Your photos

Every photo you resize is processed only on your own device. Background removal runs with an on-device AI model. Nothing is uploaded to any server. Resized photos are saved to your Downloads folder.

Advertising

Smallo shows ads from Google AdMob (a banner ad and a full-screen ad after a successful save). AdMob is provided by Google and may collect device information and your advertising ID to show ads and measure them. Smallo itself does not receive your personal information from AdMob. When you first open the app, you may see a consent form about personalised ads — it appears where the law requires it. You can learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and manage your ad preferences in Google Ads Settings.

Permissions

The app requests internet access only so AdMob can load ads. It requests no storage permission: picking photos and saving results goes through the Android system file picker and MediaStore, which need no permission.

Children

Smallo is a general tool for adults. It is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date.
